This application compares the apps installed on the device with the list present on [https://www.404media.co/hackers-claim-massive-breach-of-location-data-giant-threaten-to-leak-data/] on the Google Drive file [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Ukgd0gIWd9gpV6bOx2pcSHsVO6yIUqbjnlM4ewjO6Cs/edit?gid=1257088277#gid=1257088277]. It then shows the list of installed apps that are also present on the list.

Just launch the app and await.

On black the app name present on the list and on the phone.
With yellow background the package name of the app.
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f115bfaa-ec90-44b8-b409-f60a098d66e5)

Tap on app name and you will be redirected to the settings of android for that app

This app will ONLY compare the 2 list. It doesn't make any other comparisons or checks on the apps installed on the phone. It doesn't perform any additional security checks beyond that. It is the user's responsibility to conduct the final check on the app, the installed version, and the permissions or SDKs it requests and uses.
